AI for HR Professionals

HR work involves people, fairness and legal duties. AI can help with drafting and structure, but decisions about people must remain human.

What you do today

  • Recruiting and onboarding
  • Drafting policies and communications
  • Gathering and summarising employee feedback
  • Handling employee relations

Where AI can help

  • Draft job descriptions and interview guides
  • Draft first versions of policies and announcements
  • Summarise survey comments you provide into themes
  • Prepare onboarding material and FAQs

Where not to trust it

  • Hiring, discipline or performance decisions
  • Screening candidates: AI can reflect bias and create legal risk
  • Employment law requirements, which vary by place and change
  • Employee personal data in tools your organisation has not approved

Example workflow

TaskPrepare a structured interview guide
AI applicationAI proposes questions linked to the role's requirements
Human verificationCheck questions are fair, lawful and relevant, and keep decisions with the interview panel

Example prompts

Draft a structured interview guide for [role] with [number] questions linked to these requirements: [list]. Avoid questions about personal characteristics.

Summarise the anonymised comments below into 5 themes with examples. Do not infer anything not stated: [comments].

Draft a first version of a [policy] for [organisation type]. I will check it against local law and our rules.

Replace the bracketed parts. Never paste confidential information into a tool your organisation has not approved.

Kinds of tools

  • An organisation-approved AI assistant
  • AI features in your HR or survey platform, under your data policy
  • Document tools for drafting and summarising

We describe categories rather than endorse products. Tool recommendations will be added once reviewed.

How to verify

  • Check fairness and legality of every question and policy
  • Confirm the summary reflects the real comments
  • Protect personal data and anonymity
  • Involve legal or senior HR review where required
