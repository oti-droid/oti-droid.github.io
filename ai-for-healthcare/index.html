AI for Healthcare Professionals

Patient safety and judgment come first. AI may help with communication, administration and learning, but never replaces clinical decision-making.

Regulated field. Confidentiality and professional duties come first. Check your employer and regulator rules before using AI with client information. This page is general education, not professional advice, and should be reviewed by a practising healthcare professional before launch.

What you do today

  • Documentation and administration
  • Communicating with patients
  • Keeping up with guidelines and literature
  • Training and supervising colleagues

Where AI can help

  • Draft plain-language patient information for clinician review
  • Summarise administrative documents or guidelines you provide
  • Draft training material and checklists
  • Organise questions before reading the literature

Where not to trust it

  • Diagnosis, dosing or treatment decisions
  • Identifiable patient information in tools not approved by your organisation
  • Study results, statistics or references stated without a source
  • Anything patient-facing that you have not reviewed

Example workflow

TaskDraft a plain-language leaflet for a common procedure
AI applicationAI drafts from the approved information you provide, with no patient data
Human verificationA clinician checks accuracy against approved guidance and edits for the audience

Example prompts

Using only the approved text below, write a plain-language leaflet for patients at a reading age of about 12: [approved text].

Summarise this internal guidance in 8 bullets and quote the sentence behind each: [text].

List the questions I should answer when reviewing the literature on [topic]. Do not cite studies.

Replace the bracketed parts. Never paste confidential information into a tool your organisation has not approved.

Kinds of tools

  • An organisation-approved AI assistant
  • AI features in approved documentation systems
  • Reference tools with sources you can open and verify

We describe categories rather than endorse products. Tool recommendations will be added once reviewed.

How to verify

  • Check clinical content against approved guidance
  • Never include identifiable patient data unless the tool is approved for it
  • Open and read any cited source
  • Follow your regulator and employer policy on AI use
