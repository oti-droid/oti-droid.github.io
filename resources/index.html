Reality Check

What AI can do. What it can't reliably do. What to verify.

Summarising a long document

Can doGive a fast overview, pull out main points and organise them.
Cannot reliably doCapture every nuance, notice what is missing, or judge importance as you would.
VerifyRead the key sections yourself, and check names, figures and quotations against the original.

Finding sources and references

Can doSuggest search terms, angles and background to explore.
Cannot reliably doSupply accurate references. It can produce plausible ones that do not exist.
VerifyOpen every source and confirm it exists and says what is claimed.

Working with numbers

Can doExplain a method, draft a formula and describe what a table shows.
Cannot reliably doCalculate accurately by itself, especially with many or large figures.
VerifyRecompute in a spreadsheet or calculator before you rely on any figure.
