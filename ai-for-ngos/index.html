AI for NGO Professionals

Small teams with big missions need to save time without losing trust. AI can help with drafting and organising, but donors and communities rely on your accuracy and integrity.

What you do today

  • Writing grant applications and reports
  • Communicating with donors and volunteers
  • Summarising programme data and feedback
  • Planning with limited time and staff

Where AI can help

  • Draft first versions of applications and reports
  • Summarise programme feedback you provide into themes
  • Adapt communications for different audiences
  • Create volunteer guides and checklists

Where not to trust it

  • Impact figures and statistics, which must be your real data
  • Funder requirements, which you confirm in the guidelines
  • Beneficiaries' personal or sensitive information in unapproved tools
  • Stories about real people that you have not verified or obtained consent for

Example workflow

TaskDraft a section of a grant application
AI applicationAI drafts from the funder's guidelines and your own project notes
Human verificationCheck every figure against your data and every requirement against the guidelines, then add your voice

Example prompts

Using only the project notes below, draft a 200-word section answering this question from the funder: [question]. Mark gaps: [notes].

Summarise the feedback below into 5 themes with example comments: [feedback, anonymised].

Rewrite this update for volunteers in a warm, clear tone under 150 words, keeping all facts: [text].

Replace the bracketed parts. Never paste confidential information into a tool your organisation has not approved.

Kinds of tools

  • A general-purpose AI assistant, used without sensitive data
  • AI features in tools you already use
  • Translation tools, with checks by a fluent speaker

We describe categories rather than endorse products. Tool recommendations will be added once reviewed.

How to verify

  • Check numbers against your records
  • Confirm funder rules in the original guidelines
  • Protect beneficiary privacy and consent
  • Have someone who knows the work read it
