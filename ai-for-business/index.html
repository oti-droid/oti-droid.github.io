AI for Business Owners

You wear many hats and rarely have spare hours. AI can help with routine writing and thinking, but you remain responsible for what your business says and does.

What you do today

  • Customer communication and quotes
  • Planning and organising work
  • Marketing and keeping up an online presence
  • Reviewing numbers and making decisions

Where AI can help

  • Draft customer emails, quotes and replies for you to check
  • Turn a rough idea into a checklist or plan
  • Draft marketing posts and descriptions
  • Explain a report or contract in plain language before you consult a professional

Where not to trust it

  • Legal, tax or financial advice, which needs a qualified person
  • Prices and figures it supplies without your numbers
  • Customer or staff data in public tools
  • Anything published without your reading it

Example workflow

TaskReply to a customer enquiry
AI applicationAI drafts a reply from the enquiry and your notes
Human verificationCheck prices, promises and facts, adjust the tone to match your business, then send

Example prompts

Draft a friendly reply to this customer enquiry in under 120 words. Do not state prices or dates; I will add them: [enquiry].

Turn this idea into a step-by-step checklist with who does what and what could go wrong: [idea].

Explain this document in plain language and list questions I should ask my accountant or lawyer: [text, private details removed].

Replace the bracketed parts. Never paste confidential information into a tool your organisation has not approved.

Kinds of tools

  • A general-purpose AI assistant
  • AI features in your email, accounting and website tools
  • Scheduling and note tools, with attention to customer privacy

We describe categories rather than endorse products. Tool recommendations will be added once reviewed.

How to verify

  • Confirm prices, dates and promises yourself
  • Read everything before it goes out
  • Keep customer data out of public tools
  • Ask a qualified professional about legal, tax and financial matters
