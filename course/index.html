Take AI from something you understand to something you use well.

A 10-day group course for experienced professionals. One topic a day, one real task from your own work, and feedback on what you hand in. By day 10 you have a personal AI workflow for your role, with checking built in at every step.

What you'll be able to do

  • Choose tasks in your own work where AI genuinely helps

Experienced professionals in any field who already know how to work and want a practical, structured way to bring AI into that work, without learning to code.

How the 10 days work

One topic a day. About 60 minutes. Real tasks from your own work.

  1. Read a short explainer in plain language (10 to 15 minutes).
  2. Try the day's templates on a real task (30 minutes).
  3. Hand in one small assignment (15 minutes).
  4. Use the feedback the next morning: one thing done well, one thing to fix, one next step.

What is included

  • A short, plain-language explainer each day
  • 28 ready-to-use templates: requests to copy, fill-in sheets and checklists
  • A small assignment each day, with feedback
  • Three simple notebooks to keep: a task log, a request library and a checking log
  • A plain-word glossary and a one-page quick reference
  • By day 10: your own one-page AI workflow and a 30-day plan

What is not included

There is no certificate. The course gives you a working method and your own evidence from your own work.

Questions

No. The Guide helps you understand; the Course helps you apply. They work well together but stand alone.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Nothing requires programming.

Is it relevant to my profession?

Every module is built around professional tasks, and examples will be adapted by field. See the profession pages.

Will it teach specific tools?

It teaches skills that outlast any one tool. Tool guidance will be kept current separately.

What does it cost, and when does it start?

The price is a one-time payment of USD 19.99 (Ksh 2,600). Start dates are to be announced. The waitlist hears first.

Is there a certificate?

No. The course gives you a working method and your own evidence from your own work.

How much time does it take?

About 60 minutes a day for 10 days.
