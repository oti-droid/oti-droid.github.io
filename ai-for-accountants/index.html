AI for Accountants

Accuracy and professional standards are your trade. AI can help with explanation, summarising and preparation, but it is not a calculator and not a source of authority.

Regulated field. Confidentiality and professional duties come first. Check your employer and regulator rules before using AI with client information. This page is general education, not professional advice, and should be reviewed by a practising accountant before launch.

What you do today

  • Month-end close and reporting
  • Reconciliations and review
  • Advising clients and management
  • Researching tax and compliance questions

Where AI can help

  • Summarise long standards, policies or client documents
  • Draft plain-language explanations of variances
  • Generate review checklists and questions to ask
  • Describe and tidy the layout of messy data before you work on it

Where not to trust it

  • Calculations and totals: do the arithmetic in your spreadsheet or system
  • Tax rules, rates and thresholds, which depend on jurisdiction and date
  • References to standards or regulations it cites without a source
  • Client data in tools your firm has not approved

Example workflow

TaskExplain a budget variance to a non-finance manager
AI applicationAI drafts a plain-language explanation from the figures you supply
Human verificationRecompute every figure, confirm the stated cause is the real one, and edit before sharing

Example prompts

Here are the budget and actual figures: [data, identifying details removed]. Draft a short plain-language explanation of the main variances. Use only these numbers.

Create a review checklist for [type of reconciliation], noting which checks depend on our policies.

Summarise the key requirements of the text below in 8 bullets, quoting the sentence behind each: [text].

Replace the bracketed parts. Never paste confidential information into a tool your organisation has not approved.

Kinds of tools

  • A firm-approved AI assistant for drafting and summarising
  • AI features in your accounting or spreadsheet software, with outputs checked
  • Document tools for summarising reports, within your data policy

We describe categories rather than endorse products. Tool recommendations will be added once reviewed.

How to verify

  • Recalculate every number independently
  • Check rates, thresholds and rules against the official source for the right date
  • Open any cited standard and confirm the wording
  • Follow your professional body's guidance on AI use
