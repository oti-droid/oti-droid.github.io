Meet the creator

I'm Otieno Koduong'o, the person behind DiMe.

I started DiMe after teaching myself to prompt and work with AI, and realising how many capable people struggle to see how it applies to their own work. They don't lack ability. They lack explanations that begin with their work rather than with the technology.

I don't come from a technology background. My working life has been in hands-on, manual jobs rather than one trade, so I learned AI from the start, without a technical foundation.

I'm not an AI authority, and DiMe doesn't pretend otherwise. I'm a practitioner who tests: I try AI on real tasks, check what holds up, and say plainly what needs verifying. I'm currently building an AI intelligence brief generator that turns scattered information into material for commercial decisions. It's an ongoing project, and a constant test of where AI helps and where it needs checking.

In my own testing, AI struggled to separate fact from non-fact and to put information in context. That is why verification is built into everything DiMe teaches.
