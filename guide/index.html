Who it is for

Managers, lawyers, accountants, teachers, engineers, clinicians, consultants, business owners and other experienced professionals with real expertise and little time for hype. If you have wondered where AI fits in your work and found most explanations too technical or too breathless, this is written for you.

It is not a programming book, and it won't ask you to learn to code.

Questions

Do I need any technical background?

No. The Guide assumes professional experience, not technical knowledge.

Will it recommend specific tools?

It focuses on principles and tasks that last. Tools change quickly, so tool-specific material will live online where it can be kept current.

Is it only for people over 40?

No. It is written for experienced professionals at any age who want a practical way into AI.

Do I need the Guide before the Course?

No, but they fit together: Guide to understand, Course to apply.
