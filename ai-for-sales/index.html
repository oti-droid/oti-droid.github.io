AI for Sales Professionals

Sales runs on relationships and trust. AI can handle preparation and paperwork so you spend more time with customers, but what you promise must be accurate.

What you do today

  • Researching accounts before calls
  • Writing proposals and follow-ups
  • Keeping notes and CRM records current
  • Preparing for objections and negotiations

Where AI can help

  • Summarise public information about an account you are meeting
  • Draft proposals and follow-up emails to edit
  • Turn call notes into tidy summaries and next steps
  • Prepare questions and likely objections

Where not to trust it

  • Facts about a customer or competitor it states without a source
  • Prices, terms and commitments, which you confirm yourself
  • Customer data in tools your company has not approved
  • Messages sent without your reading them

Example workflow

TaskPrepare for a first meeting with a new account
AI applicationAI summarises the public information you provide and suggests questions to ask
Human verificationVerify the facts at source, drop what you cannot confirm, and shape the conversation yourself

Example prompts

Here is public information about [company]: [text]. Summarise what they do and suggest 5 discovery questions. Do not add facts not in the text.

Turn these call notes into a summary with decisions, open questions and next steps: [notes].

Draft a follow-up email to [role] after a meeting about [topic], under 120 words. Do not state prices or promises.

Replace the bracketed parts. Never paste confidential information into a tool your organisation has not approved.

Kinds of tools

  • A company-approved AI assistant
  • AI features in your CRM and email, under your data policy
  • Meeting-notes tools, where policy and consent allow

We describe categories rather than endorse products. Tool recommendations will be added once reviewed.

How to verify

  • Check customer facts at source
  • Confirm prices, terms and commitments yourself
  • Read every message before sending
  • Respect customer confidentiality and consent
