The DiMe Playbook

25 Ways AI Can Help You Work Smarter

Practical uses for experienced professionals. Every one comes with a note on what to verify, because AI assists and you remain accountable.

Four rules before you begin
  1. Start with your task, not the tool.
  2. Use AI for the first draft. Then apply your professional judgment.
  3. Verify facts, figures, names and sources before you rely on anything.
  4. Protect confidentiality. Do not enter client, patient or sensitive information into tools your organisation has not approved.

Where next

Want this applied to your own field? Read the page for your profession, find out where you stand with the AI Assessment, or see the DiMe Guide.

Profession-specific versions of this Playbook are planned. AI tools change quickly, so treat these as starting points and adapt them to your work.
